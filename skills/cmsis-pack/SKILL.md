## Typical workflow

1. Facts first: `$debug-access-knowledge`, then `$debug-knowledge` and/or `$trace-knowledge` (bring-up) must have produced their knowledge records.
2. `$prepare-pdsc-sequence-change` to scope the change, check inherited definitions and choose shared-versus-leaf placement.
3. `$generate-debug-description`, `$generate-debug-sequences`, `$generate-trace-sequences` or `$manage-pdsc-debugvars` for the content itself.
4. `$validate-pdsc-sequence-xml` on the proposal.
5. `$apply-confirmed-pdsc-proposal` only after the user has explicitly confirmed the edits.
